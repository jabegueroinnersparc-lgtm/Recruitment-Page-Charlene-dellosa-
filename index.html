Good questions to ask before you join

Do I need a license to join or get started?

Practicing real estate in the Philippines requires proper licensing and registration. Licensed agents are welcome. If you are not licensed yet, say so in the questionnaire and the team will explain the steps.

Are there fees or costs to join?

The team will explain all terms and any costs during your chat, so you know the details before you decide.

Is income guaranteed?

Real estate income is commission-based and depends on completed transactions. No income amount is guaranteed; the team will explain its current terms during your chat.

Can I do this part-time?

Yes. We ask for at least 15 hours a week.

Do I need experience?

No. The team offers training and mentorship. Sales experience helps, but you can learn it.

Where do leads and listings come from?

The team may share leads and listings, but availability can vary. Ask how opportunities are shared and what to expect during your chat.

Do I need to decide during the first chat?

No. The chat is a chance to discuss training, schedule, costs, and terms so you can decide with the details in hand.

What happens after I complete the questions?

The team will review your answers and contact you to arrange a short conversation about the opportunity and possible next steps.
